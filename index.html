Ringside · iPhone app for Taiwan's foreclosure auctions

Draw an area on the map.Get notified when a new auction appears inside it.

Circle a school district, the streets around your metro station or your parents' neighbourhood. When a court or the Administrative Enforcement Agency announces a new property auction in that area, your phone gets a notification. No more checking the court websites every day.

  • Notices from all courts and enforcement branches in Taiwan
  • Every feature free for 21 days
  • No account

Draw an area

Three ways to draw an area

Tap Draw Area at the bottom right of the map and pick one:

  • Draw: trace the streets you want with one finger. Let go and it closes into an area; drag the dots on the edge to adjust.
  • Radius: tap a metro station or your parents' home as the center, then drag the dot on the circle to set the radius.
  • District: pick a city and a district, and the whole district becomes the area.

You can keep up to 20 areas, each with its own name.

Filters

Houses only, under 1,500 萬 (NT$15M), with handover

Each area has its own filters: house or land, a reserve price limit, the auction round (1st, 2nd, 3rd or special sale) and handover only.

Turn on Also alert on new dates and price drops to also hear about price drops, new dates and cancelled auctions inside the area.

Notifications

A push notification when a new notice comes in

During the day the data is updated about every 2 hours. When a new notice inside one of your areas matches its filters, you get a push notification.

Every notification also stays in the Alerts tab, where you can show only new auctions or only the ones you watch.

Auction details

Reserve price, round, handover and share on one page

Each auction shows the auction date, court, case number, deposit, handover, ownership share, size and address, plus how the reserve price changed from round to round.

Plain explanations of handover, ownership share, special sale and deposit sit right below. To read the original, tap View court notice (PDF) or View Photos, which open the page on the official website.

My Areas

How many auctions each area has, on one page

My Areas lists each area's name, filters and current count. Tap one to see the auctions inside.

To keep an eye on an auction outside your areas, tap Watch at the top right of its details. You will be notified when its date, price or status changes.

List mode

Browse by district without drawing anything

Pick a city and a district to see its auctions as a list, sorted by newest notice, auction date or price.

Found a district worth watching? Tap Make This District an Area and you will be notified of new auctions there.

Land locations

Land listed only by parcel number has an approximate location

Many land notices give only a parcel number and no street address. Ringside places these at the center of the land section, draws a pale dashed circle on the map and marks the location as Approximate. Notices that name only a district are placed at the district center and are not matched against freehand or radius areas.

That is why the map shows houses by default. To see land, tap Land on the map.

Good to know

  • Ringside is not a government agency. The official notices prevail; read the original before you bid.
  • Ringside does not place bids for you and does not judge whether an auction is a good buy.
  • When a court withdraws a notice, it takes one or two updates before Ringside marks the auction as cancelled.
  • Updates and notifications need an internet connection.
