Help

This page follows the app's screens in order, followed by common questions.

Drawing an area

  1. On the Map tab, tap Draw Area at the bottom right.
  2. Pick a method. Draw: trace the streets with one finger and let go to close the area. Radius: tap the map to set the center, then drag the dot on the circle. District: pick a city and a district.
  3. The panel shows how many auctions are inside. Tap Next: Set Filters, or the redraw button at the top right to start again.
  4. Set the filters, tap Save and Alert Me, then give the area a name.

Filters

  • Type: house, land, or both.
  • Price limit: auctions with a higher reserve price are not notified.
  • Round: 1st, 2nd, 3rd and Special (special sale). You can pick several.
  • Handover only: only auctions where the court hands over the property.
  • Also alert on new dates and price drops: also notify on new dates, price drops and cancellations inside the area.
  • To change filters later, open the area under My Areas and choose Edit Filters from the menu at the top right.

Using the list

Switch to List at the top of the Map tab and pick a city and district to see its auctions. Sort by newest notice, auction date or price. Tap Make This District an Area to turn it into an area.

Colours on the map

  • Blue: first round. Yellow: second or third round. Red: special sale.
  • Squares are land (they appear after you tap Land on the map). A pale dashed circle means the location is approximate.

Not getting notifications

  • In the iPhone Settings app, go to Notifications, then Ringside, and allow notifications.
  • In Ringside, check that Alerts under Settings says On.
  • With a small area or strict filters there may simply be no new auctions yet. My Areas shows each area's current count.
  • After the trial ends without a subscription, updates and notifications pause.

Common questions

Why are some land locations approximate?

Many land notices give only a parcel number and no address, so Ringside places them at the center of the land section, with a pale dashed circle and the label Approximate. Notices that name only a district are placed at the district center and are not matched against freehand or radius areas. The parcel number in the notice is what counts.

How often is the data updated?

During the day, about every 2 hours, from the Judicial Yuan foreclosure search and the Administrative Enforcement Agency auction notices. When a court withdraws a notice, it takes one or two updates before Ringside marks it as cancelled. The time of the last update is at the bottom of the Alerts tab.

What if Ringside differs from the official notice?

The court or Administrative Enforcement Agency notice prevails. Tap View court notice (PDF) in the auction details to read the original. If Ringside copied something wrong, send us the case number with Report a Problem.

How does the free trial work?

For 21 days from the first time you open the app, every feature works. No payment details are needed and nothing is charged when it ends. The days left are shown under Plan in Settings.

How much is the subscription?

Ringside Pro is NT$120 per month or NT$990 per year. It renews automatically and is charged within 24 hours before the period ends, through your Apple Account.

What happens if I don't subscribe after the trial?

Your areas, filters and watched auctions stay, but the data stops updating and no notifications are sent. Everything resumes when you subscribe.

How do I cancel?

Tap Manage Subscription in Ringside's Settings, or open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel Ringside. You can keep using it until the end of the current period.

Does my subscription carry over to a new phone?

It belongs to your Apple Account. Open the subscription screen on the new phone and tap Restore Purchases. You will need to draw your areas again.

Can Ringside place bids or tell me whether to buy?

No. Ringside collects the notices and sends notifications. It does not place bids and does not judge individual auctions.

How do I report a problem?

In Ringside, go to Settings and tap Report a Problem. Describe what happened and leave an email address if you want a reply. You can also write to sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com.

How do I delete my data?

Write to sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com and we will help you delete the data on our server. The details are in the privacy policy.
