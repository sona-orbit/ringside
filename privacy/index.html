Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

This page explains what data Ringside (an iPhone app by Sona Orbit) sends, where it goes and what it is used for.

Data sent to the Ringside server

Ringside's server runs on Google Cloud. The app sends:

  • an anonymous device ID that the app generates at random
  • the device token for push notifications
  • the areas and filters you set
  • the auctions you watch
  • your subscription's transaction record (signed by Apple, used to check that the subscription is valid)

This is used to match new auction notices against your areas, send notifications, and check your trial and subscription status. It does not include your name, phone number, email address or Apple Account.

Location

Ringside uses your location only when you tap Center on my location, to move the map near you on your phone. Your location does not leave the phone. You can use the map and draw areas without location permission.

Notifications and maps

Notifications are delivered to your iPhone through the Apple Push Notification service. The map is Apple Maps.

Reporting a problem

When you send a report from Settings, Report a Problem, the app sends the text you write, an optional email address, the app version, iOS version, device model, language, and a random number used only to limit how many reports can be sent, to Google Apps Script. It is stored in our Google spreadsheet and used to reply and fix the problem.

What the app does not have

No ads, no analytics SDK, and no tracking of what you do in other apps or on websites. We do not sell data.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not see your payment information.

Keeping and deleting data

The data on the server is kept while you use Ringside. To have it deleted, write to sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com. We will reply with how to identify your device's data and delete it once confirmed.

This website

This website (sona-orbit.github.io) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This applies only to the website, not to the app. You can block it in your browser’s privacy settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Terms of use

Subscriptions and use of the app are covered by Apple's standard End User License Agreement (EULA): www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

Contact

Questions about this policy: sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com
